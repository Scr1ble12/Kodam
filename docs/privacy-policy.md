# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of release]_

[APP NAME] ("the app") is made by [developer name]. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **it doesn't collect any.**

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few things **only on your phone or tablet**:

- your learning progress (which letters, words and lessons you've practiced, and your streak),
- your saved letter lists and custom decks,
- your settings (theme, font, reminders and so on).

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

## Notifications

If you turn on practice reminders, the app schedules them on your device. They are not sent from a server, and we can't see when or whether you use them. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's settings.

## Audio

The app uses the text-to-speech voice built into your device to say Tamil letters and words. It does not use your microphone.

## Purchases

If you buy the one-time Full Unlock, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, card details or any other payment information. The app only learns whether the unlock has been purchased.

## Children

The app is suitable for children and is designed so that it collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared or sold.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature that needs data, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
