## Description (both stores, max 4000)

Learn to read, write and speak Tamil, one small step at a time.

Made for the Tamil diaspora: kids who hear Tamil at home but can't read it yet, parents who want to pass the language on, and anyone starting from scratch.

**Learn the script properly**
• All 247 letters plus the Grantha letters, with romanization and plain-English sound hints
• Trace each letter with your finger, from guided outlines to writing from memory
• Quizzes in both directions, with look-alike letters mixed in so practice stays useful

**Short guided lessons**
• Go from the first vowel to reading real words and simple sentences
• Each lesson takes about three minutes
• Open any lesson at any time. Nothing is locked behind levels.

**Everyday vocabulary**
• 150 starter words in topics like family, food, numbers and greetings
• See both written Tamil and the way people actually say it
• Build your own decks, and get a few new words each day

**Remember what you learn**
• Spaced repetition brings back each letter and word just before you'd forget it
• Streaks, stats and gentle reminders on the days and times you choose

**Private and ad-free**
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• Works offline. Your progress stays on your device.

Start free with the full alphabet, the first lessons and the Greetings deck. One purchase unlocks everything else, for good. No subscription.
